I'm a data scientist, and I work with the data that organizations already have but rarely look at: spreadsheets, records and research that exists only as PDFs. A 2020 IDC survey for Seagate found that 68% of the data available to businesses goes unused.

Every project starts with that raw material. The records come first, then the metrics, then a single question, and only after that the design. The method and the limits of the data stay on the page beside the chart.

<br>

## Selected work

**Child Health, Country by Country.** Under-five mortality fell in all 54 countries compared and child stunting in all 48, but once room to move is taken into account, the data shows neither catching up nor falling further behind. The page compares each country's first and latest Demographic and Health Survey across three measures, and a separate script rebuilds each headline figure from the raw data.
<br>[Case study](https://alexcorvin.io/work/child-health-by-country) · [Live page](https://acorvin.github.io/dhs-health-indicator-change/) · [Code](https://github.com/acorvin/dhs-health-indicator-change)

**AI Data Center Power.** Ten data centers hold 39% of the AI power capacity that Epoch AI tracks. The page draws the 73 sites with a power figure as circles sized by megawatts, ranks the ten largest by power and by computing, and opens each one to show its owner and users.
<br>[Case study](https://alexcorvin.io/work/ai-data-center-power) · [Live page](https://acorvin.github.io/ai-data-center-power/) · [Code](https://github.com/acorvin/ai-data-center-power)

**Contaminants in Cat Food.** The Clean Label Project screened 100 cat foods from 62 brands for more than 122 contaminants, but it hasn't released results for individual products. This study works only from the figures the report published, and it gives a panel to what those figures can't show.
<br>[Case study](https://alexcorvin.io/work/cat-food-contaminants) · [Live page](https://acorvin.github.io/cat-food-contaminants/) · [Code](https://github.com/acorvin/cat-food-contaminants)

Other studies apply the same method. [Spain Housing Prices](https://github.com/acorvin/spain-housing-prices-2026) maps the annual change in house prices across Spain's regions, [The Speed of News](https://github.com/acorvin/news-travel-time) measures how long news of eleven events took to reach American readers between 1799 and 2023, and [The Real Movers](https://github.com/acorvin/renewable-energy-shift) compares renewable energy shares across countries from 2014 to 2024. There's also a [Medicaid spending map](https://github.com/acorvin/medicaid-spending-explorer), and [Holding Still](https://github.com/acorvin/photography-exposure-times), which measures how rare slow-shutter photographs are and includes my own.
