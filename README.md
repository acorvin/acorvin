Organizations sit on mountains of information they rarely examine. Spreadsheets, old records, and research locked inside PDFs pile up. A 2020 IDC study for Seagate found that 68 percent of all business data goes completely unused.

That raw material is where every project begins. I dig into the records first, uncover the metrics, and shape them into a clear narrative. The visual design comes only after the story takes form. Every piece keeps the methodology and the limits of the data right on the page alongside the charts so the context remains transparent.

<br>

## Selected work

**Five Springs.** A picture-book data story about the children who get to turn five. A garden of 1,000 flowers stands for 1,000 births, and readers follow one seed through any of 87 countries, built from all 322 standard Demographic and Health Surveys. Under-five deaths fell in all 54 countries compared, and a separate script checks every value against the raw data.
<br>[Case study](https://alexcorvin.io/work/five-springs) · [Live page](https://acorvin.github.io/five-springs/) · [Code](https://github.com/acorvin/five-springs)

**The Tile Wall.** House prices in Spain are at a record, but after inflation they are still 10.6% below 2007. A wall of 1,482 hairline tiles shows how far each region sits below its peak, quarter by quarter, from 2007 to 2026. A separate script rebuilds every tile from the raw data.
<br>[Case study](https://alexcorvin.io/work/the-tile-wall) · [Live page](https://acorvin.github.io/tile-wall/) · [Code](https://github.com/acorvin/tile-wall)

**AI Data Center Power.** Ten data centers hold 39% of the AI power capacity that Epoch AI tracks. The page draws the 73 sites with a power figure as circles sized by megawatts, ranks the ten largest by power and by computing, and opens each one to show its owner and users.
<br>[Case study](https://alexcorvin.io/work/ai-data-center-power) · [Live page](https://acorvin.github.io/ai-data-center-power/) · [Code](https://github.com/acorvin/ai-data-center-power)

Other studies apply the same method. [Contaminants in Cat Food](https://github.com/acorvin/cat-food-contaminants) works only from the figures a screening of 100 cat foods published, [The Speed of News](https://github.com/acorvin/news-travel-time) measures how long news of eleven events took to reach American readers between 1799 and 2023, and [Still Air](https://github.com/acorvin/still-air) draws the move to renewable energy from 2014 to 2024 as a wind farm of 128 countries. There's also a [Medicaid spending map](https://github.com/acorvin/medicaid-spending-explorer), and [Holding Still](https://github.com/acorvin/photography-exposure-times), which measures how rare slow-shutter photographs are and includes my own.
